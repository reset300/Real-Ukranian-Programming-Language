# Expressions and Operators

## Arithmetic

| Keyword | Operation |
|---|---|
| `додати` | addition |
| `відняти` | subtraction |
| `помножити` | multiplication |
| `поділити` | integer division |
| `остача` | remainder |

## Comparison

| Keyword | Operation |
|---|---|
| `дорівнює` | equal |
| `недорівнює` | not equal |
| `перевищує` | greater than |
| `менше` | less than |
| `щонайменше` | greater than or equal |
| `щонайбільше` | less than or equal |

## Boolean

```text
і
або
не
```

`і` and `або` short-circuit in 0.0.2.

## Precedence

Highest to lowest:

1. primary/grouping/function call
2. `не`
3. multiply/divide/remainder
4. add/subtract
5. ordering
6. equality
7. `і`
8. `або`
